🧩 Connect the watchlist extension
  1. Download the extension below and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. On TradingView, Screener or Kite open the sidebar → Settings → Trading Journal Sync.
  4. Paste the connection code below, then enter this website's email and password and press Connect.
